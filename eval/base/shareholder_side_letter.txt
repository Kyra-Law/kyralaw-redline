{title} Side Letter

{subtitle} In relation to the shareholders' agreement of Orbital Kitchen Ltd

To: Priya Fernando, Founder

Dear Priya,

We refer to the shareholders' agreement dated 2 February 2025 between Orbital Kitchen Ltd (the "Company") and its shareholders (the "Shareholders' Agreement"). Terms defined in the Shareholders' Agreement have the same meaning in this letter.

{num} The Company confirms that the Founder shall be entitled to appoint one director to the Board for so long as she holds at least 10% of the Shares in issue.

{num} The Founder shall be a Good Leaver if she ceases to be an employee by reason of death, serious ill health or permanent disability, or if she is dismissed without Cause.

{num} The Founder shall be a Bad Leaver if her employment is terminated by the Company for Cause, or if she resigns within 24 months of the date of the Shareholders' Agreement.

{num} If the Founder is a Bad Leaver, she shall be required to transfer all of her Shares at the lower of their nominal value and their fair market value.

{num} If the Founder is a Good Leaver, she shall be required to transfer only her Unvested Shares, at their fair market value.

{num} The Shares held by the Founder shall vest in equal monthly instalments over a period of 48 months from the date of the Shareholders' Agreement, subject to a cliff of 12 months.

{num} The investors agree that the Founder may transfer up to 5% of her Shares to a family trust, provided that the trustees first sign a deed of adherence.

{num} This letter is supplemental to the Shareholders' Agreement, and in the event of any conflict between them, this letter shall prevail.

{num} This letter shall terminate automatically on the earlier of an Exit and the date on which the Founder ceases to hold any Shares.

{num} This letter is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Yours sincerely,

For and on behalf of ORBITAL KITCHEN LTD.
