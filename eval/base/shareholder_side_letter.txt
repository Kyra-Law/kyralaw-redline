SIDE LETTER IN RELATION TO THE SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT OF ORBITAL KITCHEN LTD

To: Priya Fernando, Founder

Dear Priya,

We refer to the shareholders' agreement dated 2 February 2025 between Orbital Kitchen Ltd (the "Company") and its shareholders (the "Shareholders' Agreement"). Terms defined in the Shareholders' Agreement have the same meaning in this letter.

The Company confirms that the Founder shall be entitled to appoint one director to the Board for so long as she holds at least 10% of the Shares in issue.

The Founder shall be a Good Leaver if she ceases to be an employee by reason of death, serious ill health or permanent disability, or if she is dismissed without Cause.

The Founder shall be a Bad Leaver if her employment is terminated by the Company for Cause, or if she resigns within 24 months of the date of the Shareholders' Agreement.

If the Founder is a Bad Leaver, she shall be required to transfer all of her Shares at the lower of their nominal value and their fair market value.

If the Founder is a Good Leaver, she shall be required to transfer only her Unvested Shares, at their fair market value.

The Shares held by the Founder shall vest in equal monthly instalments over a period of 48 months from the date of the Shareholders' Agreement, subject to a cliff of 12 months.

The investors agree that the Founder may transfer up to 5% of her Shares to a family trust, provided that the trustees first sign a deed of adherence.

This letter is supplemental to the Shareholders' Agreement, and in the event of any conflict between them, this letter shall prevail.

This letter shall terminate automatically on the earlier of an Exit and the date on which the Founder ceases to hold any Shares.

This letter is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Yours sincerely,

For and on behalf of ORBITAL KITCHEN LTD.
