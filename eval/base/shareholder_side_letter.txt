{title} Side Letter

{subtitle} In relation to the shareholders' agreement of Orbital Kitchen Ltd

To: Priya Fernando, Founder

Dear Priya,

We refer to the shareholders' agreement dated [DATE] between Orbital Kitchen Ltd (the "Company") and its shareholders (the "Shareholders' Agreement"). Terms defined in the Shareholders' Agreement have the same meaning in this letter.

{clause} The Company confirms that the Founder shall be entitled to appoint one director to the Board for so long as she holds at least 10% of the Shares in issue.

{clause} The Founder shall be a Good Leaver if she ceases to be an employee by reason of death, serious ill health or permanent disability, or if she is dismissed without Cause.

{clause} The Founder shall be a Bad Leaver if her employment is terminated by the Company for Cause, or if she resigns within 24 months of the date of the Shareholders' Agreement.

For this purpose, Cause includes:

{bullet} gross misconduct;

{bullet} a material breach of her service agreement which is not remedied within 21 days; and

{bullet} conviction for an offence involving dishonesty.

{clause} If the Founder is a Bad Leaver, she shall be required to transfer all of her Shares at the lower of their nominal value and their fair market value.

{clause} If the Founder is a Good Leaver, she shall be required to transfer only her Unvested Shares, at their fair market value.

{clause} The Shares held by the Founder shall vest in accordance with the schedule below, subject to a cliff of 12 months.

{table header}
Vesting date | Shares vesting
First anniversary | 25,000
Second anniversary | 25,000
Third anniversary | 25,000
+ Fourth anniversary | 25,000

{clause} The investors agree that the Founder may transfer up to 5% of her Shares to a family trust, provided that the trustees first sign a deed of adherence.

{clause} This letter is supplemental to the Shareholders' Agreement, and in the event of any conflict between them, this letter shall prevail.

{clause} This letter shall terminate automatically on the earlier of an Exit and the date on which the Founder ceases to hold any Shares.

{clause} This letter is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Yours sincerely,

For and on behalf of Orbital Kitchen Ltd.
